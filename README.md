# Wei-Jian Guo · 郭威坚

I am an astrophysicist at the [National Astronomical Observatories, Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://groups.bao.ac.cn/wsgp/tzcy/) (NAOC) and a member of the DESI Collaboration.

I study active galactic nuclei and supermassive black hole accretion through variability, spectroscopy, and reverberation mapping. My work uses surveys including DESI, SDSS, and ZTF to investigate how accretion disks and emission-line regions change over time.

### Research interests

- Changing-look AGN and accretion-state transitions
- AGN variability and time-domain astronomy
- Reverberation mapping and accretion-disk structure
- Spectroscopic surveys and emission-line diagnostics
